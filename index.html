The problem

A tenant moves out. The unit can't be listed until it's right.

Dirty floors and carpets, marked-up walls, broken blinds, a few things that don't work, and whatever got left behind. Each needs a different person, and the unit sits empty while you coordinate them.

Every day the unit sits like this is a day without rent. Scheduling separate cleaners, handymen and haulers can stretch a turnover by a week or more.
The details decide who applies. Renters who care about their home notice the condition of the one they're touring. A unit that shows clean and finished attracts tenants who will treat it the same way.
